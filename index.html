Frontiers in Psychiatry, 2026Original research

Nighttime screen use, sleep quality, and smartphone addiction symptoms among medical students

Liebig L, Balogh E, Birkás B, Faubl N, Zelko E, et al.

An international cross-sectional survey of 1,262 medical students at four sites in Germany, Austria, Hungary and Japan.

Between 96% and 98% used a screen in the hour before sleep. Screen use after waking at night was linked to 1.71 to 1.81 times higher odds of poor sleep quality.

Cross-sectional design; associations, not causes.

Questions researchers ask.

Yes. Illustrations and narration are produced with AI tools. The script is written only from your article, every number is checked against it by a person, and nothing is animated before you approve the text.

Yes. The video is yours to post on social media, your lab or journal website, YouTube or in presentations.

What if my paper is not open access?

Send me the accepted manuscript or the PDF you are allowed to share. I only use it to write your script.
